# Happy Run: design and content decisions

Owner brief: create an entire social run club website, inspired by Founders Running Club, Campus Runners and other established clubs, and upload it to `github.com/alvarovillalbaa`. The owner supplied **Happy Run**, **Madrid**, selected a **public repository**, and authorized desktop/mobile browser checks.

## Research inspected on 15 October 2026

| Source | Observation | Applied to Happy Run |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Founders Running Club](https://foundersrc.com/) | Community and human connections are prominent, with clear city discovery. | Lead with belonging and Madrid, with a single obvious path to runs. No borrowed member counts or partners. |
| [Campus Runners](https://campusrunners.de/) | Clear session types, distances/pace guidance and a strong social identity. | Distinct social and longer runs, transparent paces, and a practical first-timer guide. |
| [Midnight Runners](https://www.midnightrunners.com/about-us) | Exercise, regrouping and social time are part of a shared community experience. | Friendly conversational copy, a no-pressure welcome and an explicit after-run social element. |
| [Tracksmith Trackhouse](https://www.tracksmith.com/ai/pages/trackhouse) | Community programming is anchored by concrete schedules and places. | Put proposed times and places in scan-friendly rows with detailed panels. |

## Local grounding

Madrid's official tourism pages were used to verify the context of [Puerta de Alcalá beside El Retiro](https://www.esmadrid.com/informacion-turistica/puerta-de-alcala), [Puente de Segovia in Madrid Río](https://www.esmadrid.com/informacion-turistica/puente-segovia) and [the Puerta/Puente del Rey area](https://www.esmadrid.com/barrios-de-madrid/madrid-rio). These sources establish real locations; they do not establish club permissions, routes or event confirmation.

## Direction

Spanish-first voice; warm paper, vermilion, pale sage and a yellow smile stamp. Bricolage Grotesque brings a compact, expressive display style; occasional italic Georgia adds a human editorial accent. The layout moves from a direct three-word invitation to club principles, useful plans, a typographic pause, newcomer questions and a final invitation.

The identity, mark, copy and layouts are original. Reference clubs' photography, logos and proprietary content have not been reused. The generated hero is disclosed as conceptual imagery, not represented as actual members or an event.

## Operating assumptions

The owner has not supplied confirmed session times, official social links, contacts or an organizer identity. The site is therefore a complete launch website with clearly proposed sessions. It does not invent signup delivery, legal identities, endorsements or a community size. Once supplied, the organizer can update the centralized `club.js` configuration.
